History of chess

Chess is a game that originated 1,500 years ago in Northern India, in the Gupta Empire around 600CE. The original name of Chess was Chaturanga. It eventually spread to Persia, and after the Arab invasion of Persia, it quickly spread to western Europe.

Chess Piece Description

  • Pawn: Moves one squre vertically, or two squres vertically on its first turn. It captures it opponent by moving one square diagonally vertical.

  • Rook: Moves an unlimited amount of squares vertically or horizontally.

  • Bishop: Moves an unlimited amount of squares diagonally.

  • Knight: Moves one row and two columns, or two rows and one column.

  • Queen: Moves an unlimited amount of squares horizontally, vertically or diagonally.

  • King: Moves one square at any direction.
